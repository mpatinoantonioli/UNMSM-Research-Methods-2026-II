# Datasheet — Planned AAC Interaction Dataset v0.0

## Motivation
- **Purpose:** Create a traceable research dataset for evaluating whether a human-in-the-loop, context-aware multimodal AAC prototype supports independent functional communication more effectively than a predefined static AAC condition.
- **Created by / funded by:** Planned doctoral research dataset led by Miguel Ángel Patiño Antonioli at UNMSM. Data-collection funding source: UNKNOWN — to investigate/confirm before collection.

## Composition
- **What instances represent:** Planned repeated communication opportunities / session-level interaction records linked to a pseudonymous participant and experimental condition.
- **Core fields:** Pseudonymous participant ID; condition; activity/context; system suggestions; selection/rejection/modification; coded functional communication act and communicative function/concept; assistance/prompting level; message-selection time; fidelity/coding metadata; optional structured caregiver/specialist feedback.
- **Planned sample vs population:** Minimum four participants from one clearly defined subgroup of minimally speaking or nonspeaking autistic children requiring very substantial support. This is a bounded pilot and is NOT representative of all autistic children.
- **Current status / missingness:** NO HUMAN DATA COLLECTED YET. Final sample size, session count, exact field dictionary, missing-data rules, and subgroup coverage are UNKNOWN — to investigate before ethics approval and collection.

## Collection process
- **How collected:** Planned collection in one structured therapeutic communication context through prototype interaction logs, direct coding of functional communication acts, prompting/assistance records, rejected or modified AI suggestions, treatment-fidelity checks, and focused caregiver/specialist feedback.
- **Experimental structure:** Leading design: alternating-treatments single-case comparison of the intelligent prototype versus a predefined static AAC comparator, with repeated measurement and counterbalanced/randomized condition order where feasible.
- **Consent / ethics:** Collection will begin only after ethics approval. Written caregiver consent and developmentally/communication-appropriate assent procedures are required; observable withdrawal or distress signals will stop participation.
- **Timeframe / sites:** Future collection; exact dates, recruitment site(s), and access arrangements are UNKNOWN — to investigate and document before recruitment.

## Preprocessing / cleaning / labeling
- **Steps:** Planned: separate direct identifiers from research records; assign pseudonymous IDs; validate session timestamps/condition labels; apply prespecified coding rules for independent vs prompted acts, communicative diversity, and assistance level; independently double-code a prespecified subset; document protocol deviations and fidelity.
- **Leakage control:** Evaluation sessions must remain analytically separated from any model adaptation or personalization used for training. Any personalization update rule will be frozen and logged so future/test information cannot silently enter model development.
- **Raw data available:** Identifiable photographs, voice-related data, and raw interaction records involving minors will NOT be placed in a public repository. Public release, if any, will be limited to ethically permitted de-identified/aggregate artifacts.

## Uses
- **Appropriate uses:** Doctoral evaluation of the bounded AAC prototype; reproducibility/audit of the planned comparison; analysis of functional communication outcomes, user agency, system failure modes, and artifact refinement.
- **Should NOT be used for:** Autism diagnosis; medical/clinical prediction; autonomous inference of thoughts, emotions, pain, or intent; profiling beyond the approved research questions; training unrelated models; public identification of participants; broad population-level effectiveness claims.

## Distribution
- **How distributed:** Raw participant data are planned to remain access-controlled. Code, schemas, synthetic examples, documentation, and ethically permitted de-identified/aggregate outputs may be shared through the project repository after review.
- **License / restrictions:** Final data-sharing license, repository access level, and data-use agreement are UNKNOWN — to investigate and align with ethics approval, institutional policy, and consent language.

## Maintenance

- **Versioning:** Dataset schema, coding manual, artifact version, comparator, and DSR iteration changes will be versioned and justified. Protocol deviations will be logged.
- **Retention / deletion:** Retention period, secure storage location, backup strategy, access revocation, and deletion schedule are UNKNOWN — to investigate and finalize before data collection.
